Using AI

Generative AI may be used, as an object of investigation and as a tool.

Encouraged
  • Explore how the models function and interact with them creatively.
  • Use them as inspiration or assistance in ideation, drafting or experimentation.
Not allowed
  • Using AI as a substitute for original work: the structure, the reasoning and the understanding of the project remain yours.
Mandatory, if AI was used at any stage

A disclaimer that specifies

  • Which models have been used.
  • For what purposes: drafting text, summarizing ideas, generating code or examples.
  • To what extent the outputs were modified, verified or integrated into the final submission.

The interview evaluates the depth of your understanding. Any lack of clarity, or over-reliance on AI-generated material without proper insight, may negatively affect the evaluation. The theory questions test what you have learned: they cannot be prepared with AI assistance during the exam.
