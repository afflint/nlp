How to read the tracks

Every track follows the same structure.

1
Overview
What the track is about and what kind of research question it invites.
2
Possible perspectives
Examples of the breadth of the track: not assignments, you are free to propose something different within its scope.
3
What we expect from a project
The requirements that distinguish a good project in this track from a generic one.
4
Common pitfalls
Typical weaknesses to recognise and avoid.
5
Search keywords
Terms to find your reference paper on your own: no specific papers are suggested.
6
Feasibility and resources
What can be done with limited resources, and where the cost grows.
