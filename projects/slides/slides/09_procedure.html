How to book the interview

The rule

No preliminary approval of the project.

  • You choose a track and proceed on your own.
  • Exam dates are just for the registration of the final grade. The interview (theory questions and project discussion) is set by appointment.
  • How to contact Prof. Ferrara and submit the project is described in the Ariel web site (forum thread of the course).
Three steps
  1. Subscribe to any available date It only registers the final grade
  2. Contact Prof. Ferrara As soon as the project is finished and ready to be discussed, and after the date of your subscription has expired
  3. Set up an appointment and discuss your work Theory questions and project discussion, in a single interview
